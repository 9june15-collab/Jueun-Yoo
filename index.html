jueun yoo

Jueun is a graphic and editorial designer. She weaves thoughts and discoveries into the language of graphics and print. Her exploration remains open, unrestricted by mediums, materials, methodologies, or classifications, with the ultimate goal of creating intuitive visual communication through this practice. She developed a strong foundation in visual expression at Hongik University, majoring in Visual Communication Design, and exhibited her work Mindthegap at the 34th Korea-Japan Design Exhibition in 2025. In the first half of 2026, her experience studying at Design Academy Eindhoven allowed her to evolve her direction towards research and product.
