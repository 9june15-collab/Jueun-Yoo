jueun yoo

My design interest lies in speculating on and reconstructing the things of everyday life.My practice focuses on exploring different methodologies and mediums to bring these concepts to life visually and tangibly.

While studying Visual Communication Design at Hongik University (KR), I built a solid foundation in graphic and editorial design, alongside technical skills in machine knitting. In 2025, I exhibited my work, MindtheGap, at the 34th Korea-Japan International Exchange Art Exhibition. Most recently, in early 2026, I furthered my studies as an exchange student at Design Academy Eindhoven (NL).
